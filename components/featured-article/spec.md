# Standard content

Purpose: One standalone article or YouTube video, outside the feed. Article presentation switches between a full-width featured treatment and a compact standard feed-item treatment.

States: Article or YouTube video. Video has optional heading and subheading only; it renders no content below the embed. Featured article has optional heading, label, summary, post-style author header, and article comment/reaction counters directly beneath the image. Standard article matches feed’s single-item layout: label, headline, metadata, and optional square thumbnail. No card border.

Constraints: Feature media and video span page width; text uses normal page padding.

Reuse: Uses theme background, ink, muted, accent, and typography. Existing saved instances without a presentation value render Featured.
