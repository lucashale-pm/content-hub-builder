# Image gallery

- Purpose: Display a swipeable, Instagram-style sequence of images without caption or engagement content.
- Inputs: Optional heading; one to six images with optional descriptions.
- States: Heading omitted; one image; two to six swipeable images; blank image URLs do not render.
- Constraints: Maximum six images; square media frame; horizontal scroll snap; image order follows saved configuration; no caption, author, or social metadata.
- Reuse: Theme supplies container colour and heading font. Do not fork gallery markup by brand.
