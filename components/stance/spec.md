# Stance

Purpose: Let readers agree, disagree, or rate an editor's stance and share a response.

States: 2-choice agree/disagree mode, 5-choice spice mode, chosen response, optional source and comments. Multiple stances can sit in one unit.

Constraints: Starts with one stance; choices are capped at two or five by their selected mode. Empty content is omitted. No watch/video section.

Reuse: Theme provides page surface, ink, muted, border, accent, and typography.
